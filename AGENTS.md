## Git commit messages

- Separate the subject from the body with a blank line.
- Wrap the subject at 72 characters.
- Capitalize the subject line.
- Omit the trailing period on the subject line.
- Use the imperative mood in the subject ("Fix bug", not "Fixed bug" or "Fixes bug").
- Hard-wrap the body at 72 characters per line.
- Focus the body on why the change was made, not how — the diff already shows how.
